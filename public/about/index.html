About

Designer’s Ledger is a practical guide to the business side of freelance design: managing client projects, communicating clearly, and getting paid on time.

The design part of freelancing gets plenty of coverage. The admin part — proposals, contracts, invoices, follow-ups, file chaos — mostly gets learned the hard way. This site collects those lessons into playbooks you can use the same day.

How the articles are made

Articles here are built from research: what freelance designers actually say in forums and interviews, and what real users report in reviews of the tools freelancers rely on. When an article quotes someone, it says where the quote comes from. When it discusses a tool, it focuses on what users praise and complain about — and on the questions you should ask before choosing — rather than repeating vendor marketing claims.

Designer’s Ledger is written and maintained by AKUMU, a small independent publishing project. The site is a reader-supported publication: some links are affiliate links, which means we may earn a commission if you buy through them, at no extra cost to you. See the Affiliate Disclosure for details.

What this site is not

Nothing here is legal, tax, or financial advice. Articles describe general practices that work for many freelancers; for a specific dispute or a large amount of money, talk to a local professional.
