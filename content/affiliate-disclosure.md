Designer's Ledger is reader-supported. Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you click one of those links and buy a product or subscribe to a service, we may earn a commission from the seller — at no additional cost to you.

This disclosure is provided in accordance with the U.S. Federal Trade Commission's guidelines on endorsements and testimonials (16 CFR Part 255).

## What this means in practice

- Articles are written from research — including real user reviews, both positive and negative — before affiliate relationships are considered.
- We only link to products and services that are relevant to the article's topic.
- A commission never buys a recommendation. Where users report problems with a tool, we say so, including for tools we link to.
- Individual articles that contain affiliate links carry a short disclosure note near the top of the page.

If you have questions about this policy, you can reach us through the contact information on the [About](/about/) page.
