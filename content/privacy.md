## What we collect

- **Analytics.** This site uses Google Analytics to understand which articles are read and how visitors arrive. Analytics data is aggregated and does not identify you personally.
- **Email subscriptions.** If you subscribe to our email list or download a free resource, we collect the email address you provide, so we can send what you asked for. Every email includes an unsubscribe link.
- **Affiliate links.** When you click an affiliate link, the seller's site may use cookies to attribute the visit. Those cookies are set by the seller, under the seller's own privacy policy.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell your personal information.
- We do not collect payment information. Purchases happen on the seller's site, not here.

## Cookies

This site itself sets no tracking cookies of its own. Third-party services we use (analytics, email, and affiliate partners) may set cookies as described above. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time.

## Your choices

You can unsubscribe from emails at any time using the link in any email. For questions about your data, contact us through the [About](/about/) page.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
